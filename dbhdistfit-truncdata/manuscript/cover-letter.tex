\opening{Dear Editor,}

\begin{spacing}{1.3}
Please consider the enclosed manuscript, ``A Two-Stage Fitting Method for Truncated Stem Diameter Distributions,'' for publication as a \emph{Brief Communication}. The paper revisits the legacy truncated-diameter estimator and demonstrates how a two-stage workflow operating on complete-form distributions reproduces the best-practice truncated likelihood fits.

Using fixed-area permanent sample plot tallies from Qu\'ebec, Canada, I compare the two-stage method against classical truncated forms across multiple species/cover combinations. The submission is within the Brief Communication format (1,984 words including references, one figure, one table) and focuses on the methodological swap-out that enables analysts to retain standard software while matching truncated-density results.

The accompanying GitHub repository (https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/dbhdistfit-papers) provides the notebook, scripts, and legacy PSP tallies required to reproduce every figure, table, and LaTeX build. A preprint labelled ``under review'' will mirror the submission package on EarthArXiv upon request from the journal.

The work has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. I have no competing interests to declare.
\end{spacing}
