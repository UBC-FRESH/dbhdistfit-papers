\section{Discussion}

Across all meta-plots the two-stage estimator reproduced the fitted curves and information criteria obtained from the truncated baseline while avoiding bespoke truncated-density implementations. The complete single-stage estimator continued to underperform, particularly in bins near the truncation limits. These findings match the conclusions of the original manuscript and confirm that the simplified workflow remains valid when applied to the publicly available aggregated PSP dataset.
