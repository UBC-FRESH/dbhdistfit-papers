\section{Discussion}

Across all meta-plots the two-stage estimator reproduced the truncated fits to within $6 \times 10^{-3}$ in absolute relative-frequency units, and it consistently delivered the lowest AICc among the three alternatives. The complete single-stage estimator remained biased near the truncation limits, while the truncated single-stage (1st) and two-stage complete (2sc) solutions were visually indistinguishable in Figure~\ref{fig:comparison}. These findings mirror the qualitative conclusions of the legacy study without requiring bespoke truncated-density implementations.

Parameter estimates differ slightly from the legacy notebook for two reasons. The public meta-plot summaries omit per-plot tallies, so we assign unit weights instead of the published sampling-error weights. We also restricted the candidate set to Weibull and gamma distributions, whereas the notebook explored a wider generalised-beta family (including exponential and log-normal forms). Those choices alter reported parameters and AICc values, yet the ranking remains unchanged: 2sc matches or improves upon the truncated baseline for every species-group / cover-type combination, and both outperform the naive 1sc approach.
