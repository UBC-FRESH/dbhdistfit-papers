Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "A Two-Stage Fitting Method for Truncated Stem Diameter Distributions," for publication as a Brief Communication. The paper revisits the legacy truncated-diameter estimator and demonstrates how a two-stage workflow operating on complete-form distributions reproduces the best-practice truncated likelihood fits.

Using fixed-area permanent sample plot tallies from Québec, Canada, I compare the two-stage method against classical truncated forms across multiple species/cover combinations. The manuscript keeps the presentation concise while the accompanying reproducible workflow documents every step from raw PSP tallies to tables, figures, and LaTeX builds.

All scripts, configuration files, and processed datasets necessary to reproduce the results will be archived in the accompanying GitHub repository (https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/dbhdistfit-papers) and mirrored with an EarthArXiv preprint clearly labelled as under review.

The work has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. I have no competing interests to declare.
