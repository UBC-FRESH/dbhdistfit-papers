Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "A Two-Stage Fitting Method for Truncated Stem Diameter Distributions," for publication as a Brief Communication. The paper revisits the legacy truncated-diameter estimator and demonstrates how a two-stage workflow operating on complete-form distributions reproduces the best-practice truncated likelihood fits.

Using fixed-area permanent sample plot tallies from Québec, Canada, I compare the two-stage method against classical truncated forms across multiple species/cover combinations. The submission is within the Brief Communication format (1,975 words including references, one figure, one table) and focuses on the methodological swap-out that enables analysts to retain standard software while matching truncated-density results.

The results provide a practical path for agencies and consultants to adopt truncated fits without bespoke software, reducing bias in stand tables while keeping workflows compatible with existing toolchains.

The accompanying GitHub repository (https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/dbhdistfit-papers) provides the notebook, scripts, and legacy PSP tallies required to reproduce every figure, table, and LaTeX build. A public preprint labelled “under review” has been posted to EarthArXiv prior to this submission for transparency.

The work has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. I have no competing interests to declare.
