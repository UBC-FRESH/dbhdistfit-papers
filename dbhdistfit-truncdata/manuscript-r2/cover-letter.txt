Dear Editor,

Please find our revised manuscript, "A Two-Stage Fitting Method for Truncated Stem Diameter Distributions" (FRSC-D-25-00220R1), submitted as a Brief Communication.

In response to the post-R1 decision letter, we expanded the technical evidence and tightened the manuscript framing. The revision now includes:

1. Empirical shape-stratified robustness summaries across all 32 species-group/cover-type combinations.
2. Canonical synthetic robustness scenarios spanning inverse-J, unimodal-mid, near-flat, and right-shifted shapes.
3. Explicit discrepancy metrics (RMSE and maximum absolute difference) to quantify practical differences between 1st and 2sc.
4. Clearer bounded-claim language and explicit discussion of scope limitations.

The revised package also includes a point-by-point response and a highlighted-changes artifact.

The work has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. I declare no competing interests.
