## Reviewer #2

**1. Potential generalization issue (right truncation in broader forestry practice).**  
**Response:** We clarified that the upper bound is treated as an operational limit (observed maximum and/or management cap), not a universal biological constant. We also state alternatives when no defensible upper bound exists (left-truncation-only treatment or a location-parameter approach), and we explicitly note that interpretation depends on the truncation rule.

**Changes:** Introduction and Methods sections.

**2. Shape of observed distributions; inverse-J-only evidence is insufficient.**  
**Response:** We agree that shape diversity is central to robustness assessment. We therefore added:
1. Shape-stratified empirical analysis across all 32 combinations with deterministic shape classes and per-class win/error/failure summaries.
2. Canonical synthetic analyses across four scenario families and two sample-size regimes with replicated comparisons.

**Changes:** Methods robustness subsection, Results robustness paragraphs, appendix robustness tables (B1--B3), and corresponding machine-readable robustness outputs generated by the analysis pipeline.

**3. Practical meaning of similarity between 1st and 2sc not justified.**  
**Response:** We added explicit discrepancy metrics (RMSE and maximum absolute difference) and report their ranges in Results to complement AICc comparisons.

**Changes:** `method_comparison` table outputs and Results text.

**4. "Unfair" comparison to 1sc and request to replace Figure 1(a,c,e) with different shapes.**  
**Response:** We acknowledge the concern and addressed fairness at the analysis level rather than only at panel selection. Specifically, we now benchmark methods across the full empirical shape spectrum plus synthetic shape scenarios. We retain the main figure as representative visual examples, while using the expanded robustness outputs as the formal fairness test across shapes.

**Changes:** Shape-stratified and simulation robustness outputs, including appendix tables B1--B3; Results and Discussion text updated to make this interpretation explicit.

## Summary of major additions in this revision

1. Empirical shape-stratified robustness evaluation across all 32 species-group/cover-type combinations.
2. Canonical synthetic robustness suite across four shape families and replicated sample-size regimes.
3. Explicit discrepancy metrics quantifying 1st-vs-2sc similarity.
